About

I'm a developer who works across the whole stack — React and vanilla JavaScript on the front end, Node.js on the back end. I enjoy the part of a project where a plain requirement ("customers should be able to check out") turns into cart logic, an order flow, and an admin panel that actually manages it.

Most of what I build starts as a small, focused tool. I'd rather ship a calculator or a password generator that works exactly right than leave a bigger idea half-finished — and that habit of finishing small things well is what led to a full storefront application with its own product catalog, checkout, and support chatbot.
